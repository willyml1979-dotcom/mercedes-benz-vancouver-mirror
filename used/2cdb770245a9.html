2024 Honda Civic Sedan EX CVT — Modern Style, Smart Technology & Honda Reliability

The 2024 Honda Civic EX CVT delivers an impressive combination of modern styling, fuel efficiency and everyday comfort. Its 2.0L naturally aspirated 4-cylinder engine produces 158 horsepower and 138 lb-ft of torque, paired with a continuously variable transmission (CVT) and front-wheel drive for smooth, efficient performance. With its refined handling, spacious cabin and excellent fuel economy, the Civic makes an ideal companion for daily commutes and weekend drives.

Finished in Black, this four-door Civic EX offers a well-appointed interior with heated front seats, a heated steering wheel and a one-touch power moonroof.
