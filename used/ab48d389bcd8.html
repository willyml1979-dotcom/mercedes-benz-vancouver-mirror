Discover the commanding presence of the 2025 Land Rover Range Rover P530 SE SWB — a full-size luxury SUV that marries V8 performance with refined British craftsmanship. Under its hood lies a twin-turbocharged 4.4-litre V8 MHEV engine delivering 523 horsepower and 553 lb-ft of torque, routed through an 8-speed automatic transmission and permanent all-wheel drive for a seamless, confident drive in any condition. With its robust towing capability and confident road manners, the P530 SE SWB offers effortless power whether cruising highways or navigating challenging terrain.

Step inside and experience a sanctuary where luxury meets technology. The cabin is appointed with premium materials, including genuine wood veneers and high-quality finishes, evoking a sense of understated sophistication. The latest Pivi Pro infotainment system is accessed via a 13.1-inch touchscreen, offering intuitive controls with Apple CarPlay, Android Auto, and seamless wireless device integration. A high-fidelity Meridian sound system envelops you in rich, immersive audio, while thoughtful touches such as a panoramic glass roof flood the interior with natural light, creating an open, airy atmosphere for every journey.

The 2025 Range Rover P530 SE SWB also delivers on safety, comfort, and convenience. Standard driver aids include adaptive cruise control with steering assist, lane-keeping assist, blind-spot monitoring, rear traffic monitoring, and a 360-degree camera system to keep you aware of your surroundings. Its Terrain Response 2 system adapts the vehicle’s dynamics to suit road or off-road conditions, while dynamic air suspension delivers a smooth, composed ride no matter the surface. Automatic LED headlamps, soft-close doors, rain-sensing wipers, keyless entry with push-button start, a power liftgate, and a remote engine start round out the comprehensive suite of standard features.
