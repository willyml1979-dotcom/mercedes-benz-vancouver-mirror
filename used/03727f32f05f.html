2014 Chevrolet Cruze 1LT — Affordable Efficiency Meets Everyday Comfort

The 2014 Chevrolet Cruze 1LT combines fuel-efficient performance, comfortable driving dynamics and everyday practicality in a stylish compact sedan. Its 1.4L turbocharged four-cylinder engine produces 138 horsepower and 148 lb-ft of torque, delivering responsive acceleration while keeping everyday fuel consumption economical.

For first-time buyers, students, daily commuters or anyone looking for affordable transportation, the Cruze 1LT offers a comfortable cabin, generous trunk space and convenient technology. Bluetooth connectivity, cruise control and easy-to-use controls make it a practical choice for city driving, highway commuting and everyday errands.
