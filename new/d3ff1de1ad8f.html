2027 Mercedes-Benz S 580 Performance

At the heart of the S 580 is a sophisticated 4.0-litre V8 biturbo engine with mild-hybrid technology, producing an impressive 530 horsepower and 553 lb-ft of torque. The latest-generation V8 features extensive engineering refinements designed to provide quicker response, smooth power delivery, improved efficiency, and exceptional refinement.

Power is delivered through a 9G-TRONIC 9-speed automatic transmission and 4MATIC all-wheel drive, providing confident traction and effortless acceleration in a wide range of driving conditions.

The S580 can accelerate from 0–100 km/h in just 4.0 seconds, remarkable performance for a full-size luxury sedan

The AIRMATIC air suspension continually adjusts damping and ride height to provide an exceptional combination of comfort, stability, and control. The intelligent damping system can also use available Car-to-X information to anticipate road-surface irregularities, helping the S-Class deliver an even more composed ride.

The standard 4.5-degree rear-axle steering enhances maneuverability at lower speeds while contributing to stability at higher speeds, making the large S-Class surprisingly agile in urban environments

Next-Generation Mercedes-Benz Technology

The 2027 S580 introduces a new generation of Mercedes-Benz technology, including Mercedes-Benz Operating System (MB.OS) and the latest evolution of MBUX.

The MBUX Superscreen brings together multiple high-resolution displays, including a dedicated 12.3-inch passenger touchscreen, creating an immersive digital environment for both driver and passengers.

The 2027 S-Class also introduces next-generation driver-assistance technology and advanced digital features designed to make every journey more intuitive and relaxing.

First-Class Luxury Interior

Step inside the 2027 S580 and discover an environment designed around comfort, craftsmanship, and advanced technology.

The S580 features premium materials, sophisticated ambient lighting, advanced seating technology, and an exceptionally quiet cabin. Available Drive Dynamic multicontour front seats provide adjustable lumbar, shoulder and side-bolster support, along with multiple massage programs designed to enhance long-distance comfort

The S-Class also offers features such as heated front seat belts, advanced climate control, premium audio, and a wide range of available luxury and personalization options.
