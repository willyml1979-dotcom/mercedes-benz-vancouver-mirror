2027 Mercedes-AMG CLE 53 Coupe – Performance, Luxury and Style in the Lower Mainland

Experience the perfect balance of exhilarating AMG performance, sophisticated design and everyday luxury with the 2027 Mercedes-AMG CLE 53 Coupe, available at Mercedes-Benz Vancouver.

Powered by a 3.0L turbocharged inline-6 engine with AMG-enhanced hybrid technology, the CLE 53 delivers an impressive 443 horsepower and 413 lb-ft of torque. Power is sent through the AMG SPEEDSHIFT TCT 9G transmission to the AMG Performance 4MATIC+ all-wheel-drive system, providing confident traction and engaging performance in a wide range of driving conditions.

With a manufacturer-rated 0–100 km/h time of approximately 4.2 seconds, the CLE 53 delivers serious performance while maintaining the refinement expected from Mercedes-AMG.

Distinctive AMG Design

The CLE 53 Coupe combines the elegant proportions of a luxury coupe with unmistakable AMG styling. Its distinctive AMG grille, sculpted bodywork, performance-inspired wheels, quad exhaust outlets and athletic stance give it a commanding presence on the road.

Inside, the cabin combines premium materials with a distinctly sporty AMG character. AMG sport seats, an AMG performance steering wheel, customizable ambient lighting and the latest Mercedes-Benz interior technology create a refined environment for both spirited drives and everyday journeys.

The CLE 53 is equipped with Mercedes-Benz's latest MBUX infotainment system, offering an intuitive digital experience with voice control, smartphone integration and navigation technology.

Available driver-assistance features such as Active Distance Assist DISTRONIC, Blind Spot Assist and PRE-SAFE® add additional convenience and peace of mind on Vancouver's busy roads and throughout the Lower Mainland.

Designed for the Drive

Whether you're navigating Vancouver's city streets, heading across the North Shore or enjoying BC's scenic highways, the 2027 Mercedes-AMG CLE 53 Coupe delivers a compelling combination of performance, technology and everyday comfort.

Visit Mercedes-Benz Vancouver to explore the 2027 AMG CLE 53 Coupe and arrange your test drive.
