Compact Luxury and Versatility for Vancouver at Mercedes-Benz Vancouver

The 2026 Mercedes-Benz GLA 250 SUV is the perfect combination of urban sophistication and adventurous versatility. Designed for those who crave luxury in a compact package, the GLA 250 is the ideal SUV for navigating the vibrant streets of Vancouver or escaping to the great outdoors.

Effortless Performance for City and Beyond

The GLA 250 is powered by a responsive 2.0L inline-4 turbo engine that delivers 221 horsepower and 258 lb-ft of torque. Paired with a smooth 8-speed dual-clutch automatic transmission, this compact SUV offers lively acceleration and agile handling, perfect for weaving through Vancouver traffic or cruising along scenic coastal routes. With available 4MATIC® all-wheel drive, the GLA 250 is ready to handle rainy Vancouver days and weekend getaways alike.

Compact Yet Spacious Design

Don’t let its compact size fool you – the GLA 250 offers a surprisingly spacious interior with flexible seating and cargo configurations. The upscale cabin features premium materials, power-adjustable front seats, and an available panoramic sunroof that fills the space with natural light. Whether it’s a grocery run or a road trip, the GLA 250 adapts effortlessly to your lifestyle.

Smart Technology at Your Fingertips

Stay connected and entertained with the MBUX infotainment system featuring dual 10.25-inch displays, intuitive touch controls, and voice recognition. Use simple commands like “Hey Mercedes” to adjust settings, navigate the city, or control music on the go. Wireless charging and smartphone integration keep your devices powered and connected, ensuring you’re always in sync with Vancouver’s fast-paced environment.

Stylish, Modern Design

The 2026 GLA 250 exudes modern style with its sporty stance, bold front grille, and sleek LED lighting. Its compact proportions make it perfect for city living, offering easy parking and maneuverability while still standing out on Vancouver’s streets. The GLA’s aerodynamic design not only enhances its look but also improves fuel efficiency, giving you the best of both worlds.

Confidence and Safety on Every Drive

Vancouver’s diverse driving conditions demand intelligent safety features, and the GLA 250 delivers. Standard driver-assistance technologies include Active Brake Assist, Blind Spot Assist, and a rearview camera. Available features like Active Distance Assist DISTRONIC® and adaptive cruise control add extra peace of mind when navigating busy streets or long highway drives.
