Effortless Performance

The GLE 350 is powered by a responsive 2.0L turbocharged inline-4 engine that delivers 255 horsepower and 273 lb-ft of torque, giving you the perfect balance of power and efficiency. Premium materials, heated power front seats, and a panoramic sunroof elevate your comfort, while a spacious interior with ample cargo space adapts to your family’s demands.
